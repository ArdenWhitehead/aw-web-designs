FAQ

Questions Before Starting a Website Project

A helpful FAQ page reduces hesitation and answers the questions customers often have before requesting a quote.

How many pages do I need?

A simple business can often start with Home, Services and Contact. A stronger business website usually adds About and Portfolio pages.

What does up to 5 pages mean?

It means the package includes as many as five separate website pages, such as Home, About, Services, Portfolio and Contact.

Can the website grow later?

Yes. A website can start small and later expand with extra services, reviews, FAQs, blog articles or booking tools.

Do forms send real emails?

The current template validates the forms on the page. To send real emails, connect the forms to a form service, backend or email provider.
