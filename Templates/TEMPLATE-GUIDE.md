# Website Template Guide

This folder contains three separate website templates built from the same A.W Web Designs style.

## 5 Page Template

Folder: `5-page-template`

Best for a normal small business website.

Pages:
- `index.html` - Home page and first impression
- `about.html` - Business story, trust and values
- `services.html` - Services and package options
- `portfolio.html` - Example projects or gallery
- `contact.html` - Quote and contact forms

Why this works:
The 5-page version gives visitors enough information to trust the business without making the website feel too large.

## Condensed 3 Page Template

Folder: `3-page-template`

Best for a starter website, side business, trade business or simple service business.

Pages:
- `index.html` - Main message, proof points, key sections and call to action
- `services.html` - Services, packages and pricing guidance
- `contact.html` - Quote form and message form

Best information kept:
- Strong hero headline
- Core services
- Simple packages
- Trust points
- Contact and quote forms

What was removed:
- Separate About page
- Separate Portfolio page

Why:
For a smaller website, the customer usually needs to know what you do, why they can trust you and how to contact you. Too many pages can make a starter website feel thin if there is not enough content yet.

## Enhanced 8 Page Template

Folder: `8-page-template`

Best for a more established business or a higher-priced website package.

Pages:
- `index.html` - Home page
- `about.html` - Business story and trust
- `services.html` - Services overview
- `packages.html` - Pricing/package options
- `portfolio.html` - Project examples
- `reviews.html` - Testimonials and social proof
- `faq.html` - Common customer questions
- `contact.html` - Quote and contact forms

How the 5-page version was expanded:
- Packages became their own page so pricing is easier to compare.
- Reviews became their own page so trust signals feel stronger.
- FAQ was added to answer common questions before customers enquire.

Recommended use:
Use the 3-page template for quick starter builds, the 5-page template for standard business websites, and the 8-page template for clients who need more trust-building content and a more complete online presence.
