FAQ

Questions Before Starting a Wellness Program

A helpful FAQ page reduces hesitation and answers the questions clients often have before booking an intro call.

Which program should I start with?

If you are unsure, start with the assessment. It gives you a practical recommendation before committing to a full program.

Do I need to be fit already?

No. Programs are adjusted to your current fitness, confidence and schedule.

Can my plan change later?

Yes. Your plan can change as your goals, availability and progress change.

Can I train online?

Yes. Some coaching and check-ins can be delivered online, depending on the program.
